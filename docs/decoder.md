## 1. WHAT IT IS

The project's working vocabulary — **H-numbers, campaign nouns, doctrine shorthands, fixture
names** — currently lives in seals, `CLAUDE.md`, and the humans' memory. That is **the vigram's
diagnosis pointed at our own discourse: claims about the system living outside the system.**

**Tony's design, ratified in principle:** the glossary becomes **kant data**.
- A **term** is a GroupItem carrying a **name** and a **one-line definition**.
- The **corpus** is a group.
- A **shortcut rule action** prints definitions on demand.

## 2. THE USAGE SHAPE, WHICH IS THE POINT

A dispatch may open with a **decode line**:

```
decode: H4 H7 blastRadius
```

The shortcut fires **as preamble**, printing those definitions into the session **before the
instructions that use them**.

**Purpose, stated plainly:** Tony's seat is where every ruling routes, and compressed vocabulary
was making him **the least-supported reader of the sentences his signature makes binding.**
Rulings 1–3 (the T-0 adjudication) **needed translation after the ask.** This inverts that
permanently.

## 3. BUILD GUIDANCE — DELIBERATELY LOOSE

This is **nearly a degenerate minion corpus** — persistent entries, query-by-name, absorb-to-mint —
so whether it is implemented as a **corpus file** or a **plain kant group** is Clod's call at the
keyboard. **The precedent means this should be minutes, not a project.**

⚠ **If it threatens to become a project, STOP AND SAY SO.** It passes the campaign-hours test
**only because it is tiny.**

## 4. THREE DISCIPLINES THAT RIDE WITH IT — channel-rule grade

1. **SAME-DAY MINTING.** A term appearing in a seal or a SEQ gets its one-line entry **that day**,
   minted by whoever coined it, **transcribed by Clod either way**. Cost is near zero at mint time
   and **unpayable six weeks later**.
2. ⚠ **RATIFICATION DISPATCHES NAME THEIR TERMS** *(this one gets a WT number)*. Anything headed
   for Tony's signature carries a **decode line** covering its shorthand. **An undefined term in a
   decode line FAILS LOUD and mints the missing entry on the spot** — a missing definition is
   **a finding, not a shrug**.
3. **BACKFILL IS A CENSUS, NOT AN ESSAY.** Seed from the standing population:
   - **H1–H9**
   - **campaign nouns** — First Light · Gap B · vigram · the end-goal cycle · blast radius
   - **doctrine one-liners** — degrade-asserts-occurrence · citation-loses-to-measurement ·
     structural-vs-causal
   - **the WT rules, by number**

   **One sentence each.** ⚠ Where a term's meaning has **drifted across seals**, the entry records
   **the current binding and nothing else** — history stays in the seals; **the decoder is
   present-tense only.**

## 5. WHAT THIS IS NOT

**Not a runner. Not vigram infrastructure. Not a documentation project.**
It is **a lookup table with a print verb.**

The censusable property — *"which doctrine terms lack entries"* as a grep-class question — is
**a happy consequence to note, not a feature to build toward.**

## 6. THE SYMMETRY, WORTH SAVING FOR WHEN IT LANDS

The first campaign artifact written **in kant for the humans rather than for the parser** will be
**the dictionary the humans needed to talk about the campaign.**
